    What do 90% and 95% mean?

    The 90% core list matched at least 90 in every 100 words in each group of Hong Kong texts we studied. The 95% core list adds more words to reach at least 95. Repeated words count each time; your text may differ.

    Why aim for 95%?

    Knowing around 95% of the words in a text is a research-based starting target for basic reading comprehension with some support.

    About this comparison

    The percentage shows how many words in your text match the selected list. It does not tell you which words your class knows.

    How is my text counted?

    Each word counts each time it appears, including words without a match. Switching word options can change how many match; the total stays the same.
