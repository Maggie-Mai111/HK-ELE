    About the wordlist and its evidence

    Overall rank combines the study's General, textbook and Hong Kong corpus evidence. HK rank describes frequency in the registered Hong Kong corpus evidence. Use these measures alongside your lesson goals when selecting words.

    Earliest observed level records where a word or family first appears in the sampled Hong Kong textbooks and provides a local progression reference for planning. External level references retain their source's level system. A dash indicates that the displayed field has no recorded value.
