    How is this counted?

    Every occurrence counts, including repeated words. Compounds and unmatched words stay in the total. Highlighting does not change the count.

    Only forms included in the selected list count towards the percentage. All word results also shows separate counts with short forms and spelling variants expanded.

    The 90% and 95% list names come from the study’s four Hong Kong text collections. The percentage above is for your text, not what students know.

    Words to explain

    Tap a word in your text and mark May need explaining to keep it here.

    How to choose

    Might students need help with this use? Would explaining it help them understand the text or complete the task?

    Marked words are grouped by family, starting with the first marked use. Frequency, list membership and textbook stages are records to consult, not a decision about what students know. CPB100 only highlights words.
