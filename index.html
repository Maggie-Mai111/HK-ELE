    About the wordlist

    Overall family rank combines general English, textbook and Hong Kong corpus frequencies. HK family rank uses the ICE-HK and GloWbE-HK collections. Both ranks compare whole word families; 1 is the highest. Word-form counts show occurrences in those two collections.

    HK-ELE Word List contains 2,713 word families selected to reach at least 90% in each of four research corpora. Choose words that fit your class and text. Full database includes more word families.

    Textbook levels show the selected spelling’s earliest appearance in the sampled Hong Kong textbooks, not when students know it. US grade references use the US school grading system. A dash means no recorded value or list entry.

    How these words are chosen

    Suggestions include words first seen in textbooks after your class level, words listed in AWL or MSVL, and words outside the 1,000 most frequent HK families. Use these clues to choose what your class needs.

    Words first seen in textbooks after your class level come first. Within each group, words repeated more often come first; ties follow their order in your text. Choose Show more suggestions to see the rest.

    Repetition helps order suggestions; it does not make a word a suggestion on its own. All matched words remain available in your text.
